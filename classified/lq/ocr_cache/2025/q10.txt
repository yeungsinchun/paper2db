(b) Read the following passage about stationary waves in musical instruments and answer the questions that

How do we make musical sounds ? In non-electronic instruments such as guitar and piano, stable and
controlled vibration is usually produced by a stationary wave. If you pluck a guitar string fixed at both ends,
various modes of stationary waves will be produced and they all travel with the same speed on the string.
The mode with the lowest frequency /; is called the fundamental while the v mode, called the n™ harmonic,
has a frequency nv times that of the fundamental, i.e. m/f.
The pitch of a note is determined by the frequency of the fundamental. The relative strengths and proportions
of the harmonics determine the unique sound characteristics of different instruments. Therefore, a guitar
and a violin playing a note of the same pitch will have different qualities because of different amplitudes
and distributions oftheir harmonics.
